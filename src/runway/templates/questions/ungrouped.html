{# Warning for a question no page of a grouped survey carries. A survey presented by question group serves its groups. A question in none of them is served by nothing: the response records it as skipped, and EDSL refuses to create a human survey from the survey at all, naming the questions it would drop. So this is a survey that cannot run as written, not a preview that is behind -- which is why it takes the loud amber warning `unsupported.html` gives a type no respondent could answer, rather than the informational blue `background.html` gives a question nobody is *meant* to answer. The question text is drawn above it, in the same markup every other question's text is drawn in. Nobody is served this wording, but it is how an author will recognise which question has fallen out of the survey, and a page naming no question would be a warning about nothing in particular. Only the control is replaced: drawing one would show a page that cannot exist. Both `edsl-preview-warning` and a class of its own: the first is what makes this the same kind of news as an unanswerable type -- `check` reports both as a warning, and the two must not be able to disagree -- and the second is what tells the two apart. #}
